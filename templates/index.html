About Tariq

A smarter way to report road damage in Lebanon.

Tariq.lb is an AI-powered road damage reporting platform created to help make Lebanon's roads safer. Citizens can report potholes, cracks, and other hazards by uploading a photo. The system analyzes the image, records its location, and places it on a live interactive map so municipalities can respond more efficiently.

Our Mission

Improve road safety, encourage community participation, and give municipalities reliable information to prioritize maintenance.

Our Vision

Build a nationwide collaborative platform where every citizen can use technology and AI to contribute to safer roads and stronger infrastructure.
