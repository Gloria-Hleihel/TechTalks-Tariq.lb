About Tariq

AI-powered road reporting for Lebanon.

Tariq.lb helps citizens report potholes, cracks, and road hazards by uploading a photo and confirming a Lebanese location. The platform uses AI to analyze damage and places reports on a live map for review.

FAQ

Quick answers

How do I report road damage?

Upload a clear road photo, confirm the location, and submit the report.

What kind of photo should I upload?

Use a clear photo of the road surface where the damage is visible and not heavily blurred.

What if GPS is not available?

You can search for a Lebanese city or village, then place the marker manually.

Can I move the marker after searching?

Yes. Search helps you reach the area, then you can drag the marker to the exact road point.

Who reviews submitted reports?

Administrators can review reports, update their status, and keep completed reports for records.

Does the AI make the final decision?

No. AI helps detect visible damage, but admins can still review the report and manage its status.

What happens after a report is completed?

Completed reports are removed from the public live map but remain visible in the admin dashboard.

Can I ask about a specific report?

Yes. Add the report ID in the form so the team knows which report you mean.
